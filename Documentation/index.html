1. Product Overview & Architecture

Welcome to RecruitFlow — a commercial, self-hostable, multi-tenant AI-Powered Recruitment Management System & Applicant Tracking System (ATS) engineered with Next.js App Router, NestJS, PostgreSQL, Prisma ORM, Better Auth, Redis/BullMQ, and Tailwind CSS.

Self-Hosted Independence Guarantee: RecruitFlow operates 100% independently on your own infrastructure with zero reliance on the original developer's servers.

Core Capabilities

  • Multi-Tenant Organization Isolation: Server-enforced OrganizationGuard and 5-tier RBAC (SUPER_ADMIN, ORG_ADMIN, HIRING_MANAGER, RECRUITER, INTERVIEWER).
  • Better Auth Single Source of Truth: Email/password registration, session verification, password reset, two-factor authentication, and session revocation.
  • Complete Hiring Lifecycle: Candidates 360° profiles, Job requisitions, 9-stage Kanban & Table Application Pipeline, and Timezone-aware Interview Scorecards.
  • Provider-Independent AI Suite: Supports OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude for Resume Parsing (with human review staging), Explainable Candidate Matching, Job Description Generation, Interview Question Rubrics, and Email Drafting.
