Recruitment & ATS Modules Guide

  • Executive Dashboard: Real database aggregations for candidates, active jobs, funnel conversion, and upcoming interviews.
  • Candidate Management: 360° profiles, skills, work experience, education, certifications, notes, and versioned resumes.
  • Job Requisitions: Draft/Published/Paused/Closed lifecycle, required vs. preferred skills, and AI job description generation.
  • Application Pipeline: Interactive 9-stage Kanban Board and Table View. Supports multiple independent job applications per candidate.
  • Interviews & Scorecards: Timezone-aware panel scheduling and 1–5 criteria evaluation scorecards.
