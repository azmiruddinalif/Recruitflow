# RecruitFlow — User & Administrator Guide

---

## 1. Multi-Tenant Workspace & Role Switching
- Use the **Organization Switcher** at the top of the left sidebar to switch between organizations or create a new company workspace.
- Each member operates under their assigned organization role (`Organization Admin`, `Hiring Manager`, `Recruiter`, or `Interviewer`).

## 2. Managing Jobs & AI Job Descriptions
1. Navigate to **Jobs** → click **+ Create Job**.
2. Enter a job title, department, experience level, and core skills, or click **Generate with AI** to draft a complete description, responsibilities, and qualifications list.
3. Review and edit any generated text before saving as `DRAFT` or `PUBLISHED`.

## 3. Adding Candidates & AI Resume Parsing
1. Navigate to **Candidates** or **AI Studio → Resume Parser**.
2. Upload a candidate's PDF or DOCX resume.
3. RecruitFlow extracts the text and parses structured fields (`Skills`, `Work Experience`, `Education`, `Certifications`, `Summary`) into a **`PENDING_REVIEW`** state.
4. Review and edit the extracted fields side-by-side before clicking **Approve & Save Candidate**.

## 4. Managing the Application Pipeline (Kanban & Table)
1. Navigate to **Applications** to view applications across all 9 stages (`New`, `Screening`, `Shortlisted`, `Interview`, `Assessment`, `Offer`, `Hired`, `Rejected`, `Withdrawn`).
2. A single candidate can apply to multiple jobs simultaneously; moving a candidate's stage on one job never affects their application on another job.
3. Click the **AI Match Score** pill on any application card to view the explainable breakdown (matching skills, missing skills, experience alignment, and interview recommendations).

## 5. Scheduling Interviews & Submitting Scorecards
1. Navigate to **Interviews** → click **+ Schedule Interview**.
2. Choose the application, interview type (`Video`, `Technical`, `Panel`, `Onsite`), date/time, timezone, meeting link, and assign one or more interviewers.
3. After the interview, assigned interviewers click **Submit Scorecard** to rate technical, communication, problem-solving, and culture alignment (1–5) and record their hiring recommendation (`Strong Hire`, `Hire`, `Neutral`, `No Hire`, `Strong No Hire`).
